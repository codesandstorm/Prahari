# PRAHARI Schedule Temporal Evaluation V3

Four predeclared expanding-origin periods are retained from Prediction Research V2. Training targets must mature before validation, and validation precedes test. Imputation, scaling, calibration and thresholds are fold-local. July 2026 is excluded from every stage.

Fold admission requires at least 1,000 test anchors, 300 projects, 30 events, 500 training anchors and both outcome classes in the past training and validation partitions. Failed folds remain descriptive-only and are excluded from admitted aggregate claims.

S1 Basic and S2 Basic each admit only the October 2025–March 2026 fold after the corrected target censoring is applied. Compact admits no folds because its richer fields begin too late to provide sufficient mature training and validation history.
