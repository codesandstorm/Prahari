# Multi-Horizon Research Policy

Three months is PRAHARI's primary near-term warning horizon. Six months is a separately versioned research question with identical S1/S2 semantics and stricter outcome-coverage censoring. It cannot become an operational “6M watch” until human target transfer, event support, temporal stability, calibration, false-alert burden and useful lead time independently pass.

Twelve months is not admitted: `TWELVE_MONTH_HORIZON_INSUFFICIENT_SUPPORT`. Only 3,251 machine-provisional S1 anchors and 816 S2 anchors remain across the full history, so S2 is below even the predeclared 1,000-row minimum before temporal folding. No 12-month model is run. Metrics and probabilities from different horizons must never be merged.
