# Prospective Holdout Policy

The genuine July 2026 report is untouched prospective evidence. It is not part of the V2 dataset, feature discovery, human-transfer sample, fitting, calibration, threshold setting, ensemble weighting or model selection.

After all V2 decisions are frozen independently, the genuine July report may be processed once under the frozen source, identity, target and feature contracts. Its outcome cannot be scored until the required future months exist. Any earlier inspection is provenance/schema feasibility only, not performance evidence.
