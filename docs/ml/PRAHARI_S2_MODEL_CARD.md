# PRAHARI S2 Model Card V1

**Status:** RESEARCH ONLY

S2 is separate from S1. Eligible projects already have a revised completion date later than the original date at T. The baseline is the approved/reported date known at T; an event is a further increase in T+1…T+3 with complete project-level observation.

The research cohort has enough machine-derived events for evaluation, but those transitions have not received source-level human adjudication. The frozen artifact therefore supports reproducibility only. Operational probability and risk band are withheld.

The model must not be described as detecting causes, contractor responsibility, or all future delay.
