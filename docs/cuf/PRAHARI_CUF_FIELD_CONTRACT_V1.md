# PRAHARI CUF Field Contract V1

**Contract:** `prahari-cuf-field-registry-v1.0`  
**Authority:** MoSPI IPMD Office Memorandum F.No. 12011/08/2023-IPMD (61319), dated 3 September 2024  
**Verified source fingerprint:** `c058929cc4f19dab8df2e1527419a865036080996f60cf976da9e55a547d4412`

The attached 17-page official document contains the Common Upload Form in Annexure I, 29 major field definitions in Annexure II, the milestone module in Annexure III, the new milestone-based overrun method in Annexure IV, and the existing OCMS method in Annexure V. The machine-readable contract is `config/cuf_field_registry.json`.

The registry keeps five meanings separate: official CUF field, current historical column, frozen model feature, current Implementation Watch input, and future PAIMANA/CUF input. A mapping is never proof of current availability. The current historical source remains the 48,326-row, 3,417-project, 30-observed-month `project_month.csv` artifact.

Official families are project profile, cost/financial, funding, progress/phasing, land and right of way, approvals/clearances, tender, milestones/timeline, and promoter/implementing context. Contact details, project-completion outcomes, and future values are never prediction inputs.

The official document defines actual financial progress as absolute values with annual cumulative percentages auto-calculated against annual total capital outlay. Consequently, PRAHARI does not equate historical cumulative expenditure with the CUF scheduled-financial percentage. The financial plan gap is unavailable until compatible measures and time bases are supplied.

All dates and revisions must be those visible at the as-of date. Revised milestone dates/costs represent approved revisions only under Annexure III. Actual completion date and project completion cost are outcome fields.
