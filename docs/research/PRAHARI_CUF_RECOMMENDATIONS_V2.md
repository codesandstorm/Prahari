# CUF Recommendations V2

## P0 essential

1. Milestone baseline/revised/actual dates and status.
2. Land required, acquired and actually available to the work package.
3. Clearance type, requirement, application, decision, status and ageing.
4. Stable contract/package and contractor IDs with operational status, stoppage, replacement and termination events.
5. Issue register with category, severity, opened/closed dates, owner and escalation state.

## P1 high value

1. Approved recovery-plan and intervention log.
2. Project-level allocation, scheduled/actual fund release and utilisation.
3. Planned/deployed manpower and critical equipment/material constraints.
4. Scope/design revision with first-known and approval timestamps.
5. Reporting-correction reason and corrected-at timestamp.

Anticipated completion and cost deserve a separate A1/A2 evaluation only after their CUF meaning, approval state, first-known timestamp and modern availability are verified. Current history is incomplete and must not support a circular target.

Future Model C experiments must be incremental: milestones; then land/clearance; issues; contractor/package; funding/resources; finally timestamped external conditions. No stage has a numerical result yet.
